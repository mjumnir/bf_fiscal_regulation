Burkina Faso Fiscal Regulation

Odoo 17 — IFU, RCCM, régime d'imposition and division fiscale

This addon adds the fiscal identification required by Burkina Faso's Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI) to contacts, companies, and to the Sale Order and Customer Invoice reports.

What it adds

  • N° IFU and N° RCCM fields on contacts and companies;
  • required for company contacts, optional for individuals;
  • always required on the company record itself;
  • Régime d'imposition and Division fiscale, maintained from two simple configuration lists;
  • enforced both in the form views and at the ORM level, so imports and API calls are covered too.

On your documents

  • the Sale Order report prints the seller's IFU, RCCM, tax regime and fiscal division;
  • the Customer Invoice report does the same;
  • the customer's own IFU/RCCM are printed alongside, whenever filled in.

Usage

  1. Install the addon, then fill in the IFU and RCCM of your own company (Settings ▸ General Settings ▸ Companies).
  2. Fill in Régime d'imposition and Division fiscale for your company if applicable.
  3. Company-type contacts will now require an IFU and an RCCM before they can be saved; individual contacts are unaffected.
